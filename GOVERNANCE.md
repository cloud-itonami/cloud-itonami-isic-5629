# Governance

cloud-itonami-isic-5629 follows open-source governance with transparent
decision-making documented in ADRs.

Scope: Service-record (meal-count/menu/allergen-flag) logging, prep/service
scheduling proposals, supply-order coordination, and food-safety-concern
flagging for institutional/contract food-service facilities.

Out-of-scope: Finalizing food-safety-clearance decisions, overriding
allergen-exclusion requirements, direct kitchen-equipment actuation, and
food-safety-authority enforcement (health-department clearance, inspection
sign-off, license/permit actions).

See README.md for complete CRITICAL scope exclusions.
